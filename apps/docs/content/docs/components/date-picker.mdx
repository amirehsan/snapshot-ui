## Basic

A field for one day. Type the date into its segments, in the locale's order, or choose it on the calendar. The button at the field's end, or Alt+Arrow Down, opens the calendar on the value's month, or on `today`'s month when there is no value.

- Value: an ISO date, `'2026-10-14'`, in and out. Days only, never times.
- `onChange`: receives the ISO date once every segment is typed and the year has its four digits, and `null` once every segment is cleared. A year being typed or erased is held back: 2, 20 and 202 are never sent. Erasing the year empties the field and sends `null`, and leaving the field with a year cut short does too, so a required field then shows itself invalid. The hidden input and the face agree with what you hold. Days before the year 1000 cannot be entered.
- Forms: `name` submits the ISO date.

## Presets

`presets` adds a row of days you name above the calendar: "Today", "Yesterday". The library computes no dates: compute each from your own today, ideally on the server. A press sets the day, closes the calendar and returns focus to the button. A preset is pressed while its day is chosen, and disabled when its day falls outside `minValue` or `maxValue` or `isDateUnavailable` rejects it.

## In a review grid

A grid of fields with no label rows: each field is named by `aria-labelledby`, pointing at its column's header and its row's name, so a screen reader hears "Date Corner Mart". A changed field with no label has no chip to show, so it draws the changed dot instead: a clay dot in its frame, before its button or at its text's end, and a hidden "Changed" read after its name. An invalid field shows its error edge and line, and no dot. Compact density draws the 36px field.

## Usage

- ISO in and out, Gregorian whatever the calendar shown: a Persian page shows Mehr 1405 and still sends `'2026-10-14'`.
- Pass `today` from your user's time zone, as an ISO date. It marks today and opens the calendar on its month when there is no value.
- Pass `valueLabel` wherever the server's HTML should show the day, written like any other string in the app: "14 Oct 2026". The segments' text, order and names come from `Intl`, which the server must not write. Until hydration the field draws the label, the frame at its final size, the button, the hidden input and your `valueLabel` in place of the segments; the segments replace it without moving anything.
- In a grid, name each field with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, and pass `isChanged` when its value differs from the saved one.
- A value that is not an ISO date reads as none and warns in the console. A hand-edited URL never throws.
- Inside a [Data table](/docs/components/data-table) cell, or a list item at a narrow width, the segments keep their keys while they have focus: the arrows change and move between segments, digits type, and J types nothing. To leave the field, press Escape, which returns focus to its row, where the arrows move between rows and cells again; with the calendar open, the first Escape closes it and the second leaves the field. Tab moves on to the button and then to the next field in the table, field to field like a form, and out of the table after the last.

## Accessibility

The field is a `group` named by its label, its `aria-label` or its `aria-labelledby`. Each segment is a `spinbutton` named by its type and the field's name: "month, Date". On iOS, each segment is a text box. The button, named "Choose date" and the field's name, opens a `dialog` with the calendar, and the presets are a group named "Presets".

"Choose date", "Previous month", "Next month" and "Presets" come from `labels`. Segment type names come from the browser. React Aria's announcements, such as the chosen date, have no Persian.

A changed field with no label is described by a hidden "Changed", from `changedLabel`, which leaves while the field is invalid.
