## Free content

A popover opens beside its trigger, with the float fill, frame, surface corner and float shadow. Menus and lists of options open in it, and it also holds free content, such as a filter panel whose choices apply as they change. `isPadded` pads free content 24px. It is off by default, because a list or a menu pads itself.

## Matching the trigger

`matchTriggerWidth` makes the trigger's width the popover's minimum, so a panel under a wide trigger lines up with both edges. Wider content still widens the popover.

## Usage

Wrap free content and its button in a `DialogTrigger`. Or control the popover with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange`, and point `triggerRef` at the element it opens from. A menu, a select or a combo box brings its own trigger, so do not wrap it in a `DialogTrigger`. Popover is a client component that a server component can render, as the examples on this page do.

It opens 8px under its trigger at the start edge. `placement` and `offset` change that: `placement="top end"` opens it above the trigger at the end edge.

- Fitting: with no room on its side, it flips to the other side, and it shifts along the trigger to stay 12px inside the window.
- `data-placement`: names the side the popover landed on.
- Size: never wider than the screen less 2rem. Taller content scrolls inside it.
- Right to left: `start` and `end` follow the writing direction.
- No arrow: the gap and the shadow tie it to its trigger.

A popover takes the theme, shape and density of its trigger, so one opened from a dark card is dark on a light page. See [Theming](/docs/theming#overlays).

## Props

`ref` and `className` go on the popover element, the one that takes focus. `className` is merged after the library's classes. `triggerRef` is the element it opens against. A trigger component hands it over. Outside one, a popover opened with `isOpen` or `defaultOpen` needs it, or it throws an error that names `triggerRef`. The rest of React Aria's popover props pass through.

## Accessibility

Opened from a `DialogTrigger`, the popover is a dialog named by its trigger. Focus moves into it, and Tab cycles through its controls without reaching the page. Escape or a press outside closes it and returns focus to the trigger. While it is open, the page behind is hidden from assistive technology and does not scroll.
