## Navigation

A drawer is a panel that slides in from the start or end edge, full height and floating 16px inside the viewport. It holds a header with the title and a close button, a body, and a pinned footer. The body scrolls when it is too tall, and the header and footer stay.

- `title` is required, because it names the drawer.
- `isTitleHidden` keeps the title for assistive technology only, for content that names itself, such as a navigation list. The header shows just the close button.
- With `hasCloseButton={false}` as well, there is no header, and the body starts at the top.
- `footer` holds what should stay in view, such as the signed-in account or an action.
- A scrolling body with nothing to focus, such as a page of notes, is a tab stop of its own, so the keyboard can scroll it.

## From the end

`placement="end"` brings the panel from the other edge, for a filter form beside a list. Put its action in the footer to keep it in view. A button with `slot="close"` closes the drawer it is in.

## Usage

Open a drawer from a `DialogTrigger`, which holds the opening button and the drawer. Or control it with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange`, or start it open with `defaultOpen`. `children` can be a function that receives `close`, for a link that closes the drawer as it navigates. Drawer is a client component, and a server component can render it, as the examples here do.

The drawer closes only when asked. An app shell that shows a rail on wide screens and a drawer on narrow ones controls it with `isOpen`, and closes it when the viewport widens past its breakpoint.

- Size: 20rem wide at most, and never wider than the screen less 2rem. It floats 16px from every edge, like the toast and a rail, with the dramatic modal shadow.
- Direction: `start` and `end` follow the writing direction. In right to left, the start is the right edge.
- Theme: a drawer opened from a dark card is dark while the page stays light, as [Theming](/docs/theming#overlays) explains.

## Props

`ref` goes on the dialog element, the one that takes focus. Unlike Dialog and Sheet, a drawer passes no other React Aria dialog props through, such as `aria-describedby`, `id` or `aria-label`. Its title names it, and anything else goes in its body. `className` goes on the panel and `overlayClassName` on the scrim, each merged after the library's classes. A class on the panel may restyle `.drawer-body` and `.drawer-footer`, such as to set your own padding. The header and other inner parts may change between releases.

## Accessibility

The drawer is a dialog named by its title, even a hidden one. When it opens, focus moves in, and Tab cycles inside it without reaching the page. When it closes, focus returns to the button that opened it. While it is open, the page behind is hidden from assistive technology and does not scroll.
