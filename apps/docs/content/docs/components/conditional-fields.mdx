## Kept, but not sent

A setting that needs more once it is on, such as bank logos that need a provider and a key, shows those fields under its switch and hides them when the switch turns off. While hidden, the fields keep what the reader typed: nothing clears them, so turning the switch back on brings everything back as it was.

Hidden fields are left out of the form's submission and its checks. A hidden key never overwrites the saved one, and a hidden required field never blocks a save with an error nobody can see. So an absent field means nothing on its own: read the gate on the server to know what it means. With logos off, keep the saved key; with logos on and no key, ask for one.

The group is a fieldset that is `hidden`, `inert` and `disabled` while the gate is off, in the server's HTML too, so before any script runs a hidden field is out of the tab order, the reading order and the submission. Pass `submitsWhenHidden` for the other model, where hidden fields are still sent and checked and your server ignores them by the gate; then make those fields not required yourself.

</Section>

## The gate

The gate is your own control, usually a [switch](/docs/components/switch), placed right before the group, with `aria-controls` naming the group's `id`. The fields line up with the switch, with no indent, and appear at once.

## Usage

A client component, for its focus move. It renders the group shown or hidden from `isShown` on the server, and never reads or writes a value.

```tsx
import { ConditionalFields, Switch, TextField } from '@snapshot-ui/react';

<>
  <Switch isSelected={logo} onChange={setLogo} aria-controls="logo-fields" name="logos">
    Show bank logos
  </Switch>
  <ConditionalFields id="logo-fields" isShown={logo}>
    <TextField label="API key" name="logoKey" type="password" />
  </ConditionalFields>
</>;
```

Space the group as your form spaces its fields with `--conditional-fields-gap`, which defaults to the peer gap. A changed field keeps its Changed chip while hidden; if your SaveBar counts changes, count only what will be saved, which with the default is what shows.

## Accessibility

The gate says its own state, checked or not, and names the group with `aria-controls`; `aria-expanded` is not allowed on a switch. Nothing is announced when the group shows: the reader on the gate hears it turn on, and the fields follow it in the reading and tab order. Turning the gate off keeps focus on the gate. If the group hides while focus is in it, because another control or your app turned the gate off, focus goes to the gate, else to the control before the group, else to `<main>`, never the body. Without a name the group has `role="none"`, since the gate before it says what follows; pass `aria-label` for a named group. In development, a group no gate points at warns once.
