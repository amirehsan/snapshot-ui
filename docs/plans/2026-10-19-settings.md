**Architecture:**

- **The URL is the state.** PanelLayout holds no section: the app passes `view` and `sectionKey` from its address, marks the open link `isCurrent` and the index's section `isDefault`, and the layout draws both faces in one HTML, one shown by a container query on its own box (spec 4, 5.4, 5.5).
- **Focus goes to a named place, after the commit.** Every part that removes or hides what holds focus moves it, in a frame after React's commit and never in a layout effect, to a place the reader can name: a section's heading, the link of the section left, the next row's first field, Add, the gate, the field an offer set, the menu's trigger. One internal module, `utils/focus-after-commit.ts` (Task 3), holds the frame, the focus watch that survives Chromium's early blur, and the "never the body" fallback; Tasks 4 to 7 reuse it (spec 4, 12.4; lesson 10).
- **Forms submit what the reader sees.** A hidden group is a hidden, inert, disabled fieldset; a Repeater's rows submit in drawn order under the app's names, or as the app's typed rows through `onChange`; an offer is the app's setter, never applied by itself (spec 7.4, 6.7, 8.2; rulings 2.5, 2.6, 3).
- **Compose what exists.** ThemeSwitch is SegmentedControl or RadioGroup; ThemeMenu is Menu's own selection in a submenu or a section; the account trigger draws Avatar; CopyButton is IconButton or Button with a Tooltip; FieldExtras puts a tertiary Button under anything. No existing field changes its API (spec 4).
- **Static where nothing moves.** PanelSection and PanelRow are server parts with no directive; every client part renders its whole face on the server and reads nothing from `window` at render (spec 15).
- Every task is one reviewable deliverable; the order puts the small independent parts first, so each later task composes parts that already exist (ruling 1), and a last task for the recipe, the guides, the charts peer and the batch's baseline list.

## Global Constraints

- Node `>=24.5`, `"packageManager": "pnpm@10.10.0"`, every package `"type": "module"`, ESM only.

Client parts warn once in development through `useWarnOnce` (`utils/useWarnOnce.ts`): a Repeater or a CopyButton whose status has an `aria-busy="true"` ancestor when it speaks, a Repeater with two rows of one id, a ConditionalFields group no gate points at. The static parts, PanelSection and PanelRow, cannot warn: three children in a PanelRow is a type error, and at run time it draws what it holds.

- **Focus moves after the commit, in a frame.** No part here calls `focus()` in a layout effect, in render or in an effect's body directly: it schedules the move with `afterCommit` (Task 3), a `requestAnimationFrame` cancelled on unmount, so a NumberField that held focus never commits inside React's commit (spec 12.4).

The widths 16rem, 30rem, 48rem and 66rem are literals in their files, as SidePanel's 24rem is (spec item 26). ### Design rules

- **Theme axes:** light and dark, the three shapes, compact density. Rows, fields and buttons follow `--size-control` and `--size-field`; stories that hold a card, a list face or a rail take the `density` tag.
- **Typography:** weights 400, 450, 500 and 700 only. A section's heading 500 at the h3 step; a nav name 500; a list face description 450 at the small step; a Repeater legend 700 at the h4 step and a row legend 500 at the small step; an offer 450 at the small step; the account name 500 and its detail 450.
- **Reserved colors.** Signal orange appears nowhere. Clay is only the Changed chip and the changed edge, as fields draw them (a Repeater's legend chip, a field the offers edit); an offer is never clay. Error is only a field's, a row's or a set's invalid value; the danger zone is a plain Card with a danger Button, never a red card (open question 25). Success and gain appear nowhere, not on a "Copied" check (a copy is not a save). Taupe is only placeholders and disabled text, so a disabled Add's label is taupe as Button draws it and its reason is slate words, and a suggestion is never a placeholder. State is never color alone: a nav item's state is words, an offer and an error have words, a theme choice has its word and its check.
- **The concentric rule** (design record, "Nested radii, derived"): a surface in a holder's corner takes the holder's radius less the gap; a control keeps its shape and moves out, down where a column stands above it. Here: a Repeater's Add at a card's foot and its first Remove at a card's head with no header (Task 5), a field's last offer at a card's foot (Task 4), Back in a card's corner (Task 6), the nav's row in a card (Task 6), the account trigger's wash in the rail's foot (Task 7). The audit runs on every new story in three shapes.
- **Contrast records**, light then dark. Gated already: ink, slate and the error color as text on canvas, lifted, float and raised; taupe disabled text, exempt; the boundary against canvas and lifted (3.06 and 6.20, 3.36 and 5.59); canvas on ink (the current pill). No new pair: each task's `contrast.test.js` describe cites the gated pairs it uses and recomputes, to two decimals, every value its Appendix B record states.

### Standing lessons from batches 4 to 6

Each was learned the hard way and holds for every task here. Lessons 1 to 8 are copied from the accounts plan and lessons 1 to 7 from the insights plan, which are the same seven; where a lesson names an earlier batch's part, this batch's equivalent follows in brackets. Lessons 9 to 14 are new to this plan.

1. **The concentric corner rule** (design record, "Nested radii, derived"; batch 4's commits 3080858, d48f170 and d57b1fc). A surface in a holder's corner takes `calc(var(--holder-radius) - var(--holder-border) - var(--holder-pad))` on every corner; a control keeps its shape and stops at `--holder-corner-inset`, moving down where a column stands above or below it. Story code places an app's controls with `CLEAR_OF_FOOT` and surfaces with `HOLDER_CORNER` (`src/stories/corner.ts`), and so do the docs examples. The audit (`apps/storybook/tests/concentric`) runs every story in three shapes and fails a control left in a curve; the docs audit (`apps/docs/tests/concentric.spec.ts`) does the same for every docs page, the docs header included. [Here: a Repeater's Add and first Remove, a field's last offer, Back and a nav row in a card, the account trigger in the rail's foot; each corner selector joins the shared `CLEAR` list in `css.test.js`.]
2. **Focus never falls to the body** (batch 4's corrections 54, 102, 103, 111 and 113). When the focused element leaves, focus goes to a named place: the next control, the trigger, a heading with `tabIndex={-1}`, or `<main>`. [Here: every row of spec 13's list, each with a play in three engines and a docs end-to-end read of `document.activeElement`; lesson 10 says why jsdom cannot prove it.]
3. **Plays that hold on CI.** CI runs four story projects side by side: `storybook`, `storybook-dark`, `storybook-webkit` and `storybook-firefox` (`apps/storybook/vitest.config.ts`). WebKit and Firefox do not run on the implementer's machine; each task pushes and reads the draft pull request's results before it is done (owner action 1). A story test gets 60 seconds on CI and 15 locally (`apps/storybook/vitest.config.ts:12`, batch 4's correction 123). A play draws the least that proves its point; a play whose story is about what follows focus focuses with `focusOn(element)` (`src/stories/focus.ts`), which polls until focus holds, since Firefox can let an early focus go unheard; every check that follows a resize, a key, a press or a frame waits with `waitFor` (five seconds where a busy runner is slow); every pixel comparison allows 1px for an engine's rounding (`expectSameEdge` and `expectInside` in `rtl/RightToLeft.stories.tsx:107-117`, correction 126); a play that resizes or scrolls puts it back in `finally`. [Charts' stories cannot import the react package's `focus.ts`; batch 6 copied it to `packages/charts/src/stories/focus.ts`. No story here is in charts.]

**Behavior:**

1. **The deferral.** In `NumberFieldInput`'s memo, replace the context's `onBlur` with a handler that captures the event's `target` and `currentTarget` (React clears `currentTarget` after dispatch, and React Aria's `useFocus` compares the two) on an object whose prototype is the event, and calls React Aria's handler with it in `queueMicrotask`. Nothing else in the props changes.

```ts
// React Aria commits the typed text on blur inside flushSync (react-aria 3.53.1, useNumberField.mjs:60-76), which
// React refuses inside its own commit. One microtask later the commit runs after React's, or at once after a
// browser's own blur, before the next element's focus event and before a following click.
const deferBlur =
  (onBlur: FocusEventHandler<HTMLInputElement> | undefined): FocusEventHandler<HTMLInputElement> | undefined =>
    onBlur === undefined
      ? undefined
      : (event) => {
          const held = Object.create(event, {
            target: { value: event.target },
            currentTarget: { value: event.currentTarget },
          }) as FocusEvent<HTMLInputElement>;
          queueMicrotask(() => {
            onBlur(held);
          });
        };
```

2. **What an app sees.** Its `onBlur` and `onFocusChange(false)`, which React Aria chains into the same handler, run one microtask later. A pointer or keyboard submit after the blur reads the committed value; a `requestSubmit()` called by script in the same task as the blur could read it before the commit, which the page and the changeset say (spec risk R5).
3. **The Enter shortcut** (`useNumberField.mjs:167-175`) runs from a key handler, where `flushSync` is allowed, and is not touched.
4. **The sign button's own `flushSync`** (`NumberField.tsx:321`) runs in a press handler and is not touched.

**Behavior:**

1. **Markup.** `span.copy-button[data-look]` (`className` merged after) holding the button and `span.copy-button-status[role="status"][aria-live="polite"]`, empty in the server's HTML. 2. **The glyphs.** Both `Copy` and `Check` are drawn, `span.copy-button-glyph[data-glyph="copy"|"check"]`, `aria-hidden`; the stylesheet shows one by the root's `data-outcome` (`copied` shows the check; `failed` and none show the copy glyph), so the server's HTML is the rest state.

6. **Focus** stays on the button: nothing leaves.
7. **Server.** Nothing reads the clipboard or `navigator` until a press.

```ts
'use client';
export interface RepeaterRow {
  /** Stable across adds, removes and saves: the app's id, or one `createRow` made. */
  id: Key;
}

export interface RepeaterRowRender<T extends RepeaterRow> {
  /** The row's place, from 0, as drawn now. */
  index: number;
  /** The row's legend as drawn: `rowLabel(index)`. */
  label: string;
  /** The row's error, from `rowErrors`, so its fields can mark themselves invalid. */
  error: ReactNode | undefined;
  /** Writes this row back into the rows, by its id: `update({ ...row, amount })`. Calls `onChange`. */
  update: (row: T) => void;
}

export interface RepeaterLabels {
  /** Add's text. @default 'Add a row' */
  add: string;
  /** Remove's visible text; its name adds the row's legend: "Remove, Tier 2". @default 'Remove' */
  remove: string;
  /** Add's description under the maximum: "2 of 3". @default (count, max) => `${count} of ${max}` */
  count: (count: number, max: number) => string;
  /** Beside the disabled Add at the maximum, and its description. @default (max) => `${max} of ${max}. Remove one to add another.` */
  full: (max: number) => string;
  /** In the rows' place when there are none. @default 'None yet.' */
  empty: string;
  /** Said politely after an add. @default (label) => `${label} added` */
  added: (label: string) => string;
  /** Said politely after a remove. @default (label) => `${label} removed` */
  removed: (label: string) => string;
}

export type RepeaterBaseProps<T extends RepeaterRow> = Omit<
  HTMLAttributes<HTMLFieldSetElement>,
  'children' | 'onChange' | 'defaultValue' | 'aria-label' | 'aria-labelledby'
> & {
  /** The set's legend: "Wait tiers". */
  label?: ReactNode;
  /** A hint under the legend. */
  description?: ReactNode;
  /** Controlled: the rows, in order. */
  value?: readonly T[];
  /** Uncontrolled: the first rows. @default [] */
  defaultValue?: readonly T[];
  /** Every add, remove and `update`, with the new rows. */
  onChange?: (rows: T[]) => void;
  /** Makes the row Add appends, with a new id. */
  createRow: () => T;
  /** Draws one row's fields. */
  children: (row: T, render: RepeaterRowRender<T>) => ReactNode;
  /** Each row's legend, from its place: (index) => `Tier ${index + 1}`. */
  rowLabel: (index: number) => string;
  /** Rows Remove stops at. @default 0 */
  minRows?: number;
  /** Rows Add stops at. Required: a repeater is a short list. */
  maxRows: number;
  /** An error about the whole set. */
  errorMessage?: ReactNode;
  /** Errors by row id, drawn under the row's fields. */
  rowErrors?: Readonly<Record<string, ReactNode>>;
  /** Whether the set differs from the saved one: the Changed chip beside the legend. */
  isChanged?: boolean;
  changedLabel?: string;
  /** Adds and removes are refused; the fields are the app's to disable. */
  isDisabled?: boolean;
  labels?: Partial<RepeaterLabels>;
  ref?: Ref<HTMLFieldSetElement>;
};

export type RepeaterProps<T extends RepeaterRow> = RepeaterBaseProps<T> & AccessibleName<'label'>;
export function Repeater<T extends RepeaterRow>(props: RepeaterProps<T>): ReactElement;
```

**Behavior:**

1. **Markup, in DOM order.** `fieldset.repeater` (rest props, `className`, `ref`; `data-full` at the maximum, `data-empty` with no rows) with `legend.repeater-legend` (the label and, while `isChanged` and no `errorMessage`, the Changed chip), `p.repeater-description`, `p.repeater-error` (the set's error, `--color-error`); then, with rows, `ol.repeater-rows` of `li.repeater-row[data-row-id]`, each holding `fieldset.repeater-row-set` (`aria-describedby` the row error's id while it has one; `data-invalid`) with `legend.repeater-row-legend` (`rowLabel(index)`), `div.repeater-row-fields` (the size container) holding `div.repeater-row-grid` with `div.repeater-row-main` (the app's nodes from `children(row, render)`) and `div.repeater-row-end` (Remove), then `p.repeater-row-error`; with no rows, `p.repeater-empty` (`labels.empty`) in the list's place; then `div.repeater-foot` with Add and `span.repeater-count` (under the maximum) or `span.repeater-full` (at it); then `div.repeater-status[role="status"][aria-live="polite"]`, empty in the server's HTML. The set's `aria-describedby` names the description and the set's error.
2. **The value.** `useControllableState(value, defaultValue ?? [], onChange)`. Add calls `createRow()` and sets `[...rows, row]`; Remove sets the rows without that id; `update(row)` replaces the row with that id. Controlled, the app's `onChange` decides; uncontrolled, the Repeater holds the rows (ids and the values it was given), the fields hold what the reader types, and the app names them for `FormData.getAll`. The Repeater never reads a field.
3. **Ids.** React's `key` is the row's id. Two rows with one id warn once in development and draw both.
4. **Remove** is a tertiary Button, text `labels.remove` then `span.repeater-remove-name` (clipped) with `", "` and the row's legend: "Remove, Tier 2". It is not drawn while `rows.length <= minRows`; the end column keeps its width. It asks nothing.
5. **Add** is a secondary Button with `Plus` and `labels.add`. Under the maximum its `aria-describedby` is `span.repeater-count`, `labels.count(rows.length, maxRows)`, in slate. **At or above the maximum** (ruling 2.3) Add is `isDisabled`, still drawn, and `span.repeater-full`, `labels.full(maxRows)`, in slate, takes the count's place and is Add's description. With `isDisabled` on the Repeater, Add and Remove are disabled and the count stays.
6. **Empty** (ruling 2.2): with no rows the list is not drawn and `p.repeater-empty` holds `labels.empty`, slate, then the foot with Add.
7. **Rows above `maxRows`** are all drawn; nothing is cut.
8. **Focus and the status** (spec 6.4, ruling 2.3), each move through `afterCommit`, the target found by `[data-row-id]` and `firstFocusable`, else Add, through `focusNamedPlace`; a field marked `data-autofocus` in a row wins:

| After | Focus goes to | The status says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add, under the maximum after it | The new row's first field | `labels.added(rowLabel(newIndex))` |
| Add that reaches the maximum | The new row's first field | `labels.added(rowLabel(newIndex))`, a full stop and a space, then `labels.full(maxRows)`: "Tier 3 added. 3 of 3. Remove a tier to add another." |
| Remove, a row with rows after it | The first field of the row now in its place | `labels.removed(oldLabel)` |
| Remove, the last of several | The first field of the new last row | `labels.removed(oldLabel)` |
| Remove, the only row | Add | `labels.removed(oldLabel)` |

The status text is cleared and rewritten on each event, so a repeated sentence is said again (SaveBar's way). Before writing, a development warning names a Repeater whose status has an `aria-busy="true"` ancestor (lesson 11). Nothing moves on the server's render or on hydration.
9. **Errors.** A row's error is `p.repeater-row-error` in the error color, named in the row fieldset's `aria-describedby` and passed to the render function as `error`. The set's error is under the legend; while it shows, the Changed chip steps aside. Never a colored row.
10. **The default set** (ruling 2.6) is not the Repeater's: the app wraps it in `FieldExtras scope="group"` whose `onTake` sets the default rows with new ids; FieldExtras' focus rule lands on the first row's first field.

**Behavior:**

1. **Markup.** `div.panel-layout[data-view]` (rest props, `className`, `ref`; the size container) holding `div.panel-layout-frame` (the grid), which holds `div.panel-layout-side` with `nav`, then `div.panel-layout-main` with `a.panel-layout-back` (a library `Link` to `backHref`: `ChevronLeft`, hidden, then `backLabel`, its whole name) and the children.
2. **PanelNav** is `nav.panel-nav` (named by the app's `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, `AriaName`) holding `ul.panel-nav-list` of the children. **PanelNavItem** is `li.panel-nav-item` holding a library `Link` `a.panel-nav-link` (the rest of `LinkProps`, `href`) with `aria-current="page"` when `isCurrent`, `data-default` when `isDefault` (never `aria-current`), the icon (`span.panel-nav-icon`, hidden), `span.panel-nav-name` (the children), `span.panel-nav-description` and `span.panel-nav-end` with ids, the link's `aria-describedby` naming the end then the description, and `span.panel-nav-chevron` (`ChevronRight`, hidden).
3. **PanelSection** (static) is `section.panel-section` (rest, `className`, `ref`) with `aria-labelledby` its heading's id (`useId`), `h2.panel-section-heading` (or `h3` by `headingLevel`) with `tabIndex={-1}`, `p.panel-section-description`, then the children.
4. **PanelRow** (static) is `div.panel-row[data-layout]` (rest, `className`, `ref`; the size container) holding `div.panel-row-grid` with the two children, each in `div.panel-row-cell`.
5. **The faces** are CSS only (below): nothing is measured at render; `display: none` takes the hidden face out of the tab order and the accessibility tree.
6. **Focus on a section change** (spec 5.6). The layout watches focus with `useFocusWatch` on its root. After hydration, when `view` or `sectionKey` changes from the last committed values, and the watch held focus or `focusIsLost()`, `afterCommit` moves it:
   - to `section` view: the open section's heading (`.panel-layout-main .panel-section-heading`), on both faces;
   - to `index` view: on the list face (the side column has a box, `getClientRects().length > 0`, read in the frame), the nav's link whose `href` is the one noted as current before the change, else the nav's first link; on the split face, the default section's heading.
   A navigation the reader started outside the layout (focus on the rail or in a command palette, the watch not held and focus not lost) moves nothing. After each commit the layout notes the `href` of the nav's `aria-current="page"` link (plan open question 7).
7. **First render** moves nothing, on the server or on hydration (`useHydrated`). **A layout mounted after hydration** with a `sectionKey` different from the last one a layout in this window committed (a module-level note, written on commit and kept across unmounts) counts as a change.
8. **Back's name** is `backLabel` alone; the chevron is hidden.
