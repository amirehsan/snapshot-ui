Menu exports SubmenuTrigger: put a MenuItem and a Menu inside it for a submenu, opened by Arrow Right (Arrow Left in right to left); the item draws a chevron after its end value and keeps its wash while the submenu is open. The submenu opens beside its row with its first row level with it, and under the row where neither side has room. A Menu with no `placement` now takes its trigger's, which is still `bottom start` under a MenuTrigger.
