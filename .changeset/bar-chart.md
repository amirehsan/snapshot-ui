Add BarChart, with horizontal bars for a ranked list: pass `orientation="horizontal"`, since the default is vertical (see the vertical changeset). Each row has its label at the start, its bar from a zero line and its amount in a column at the end, so amounts align. Refunds that net a row below zero draw toward the start and keep their amount in the column. Long names wrap and never rotate; under 24rem of the chart's own width the names move above their bars. The chart is a size container, so its holder gives it a width: a block its whole line, a flex item a basis such as `flex: 1 1 20rem`. The chart is one tab stop on the active bar, and the arrow keys move bar to bar; each bar is named by its table cells, its category and its value, and its series when there are two or more. A toggle shows the same figures as a table. `status` draws loading, empty and error in the chart's own space, with `statusLabels` and `statusContent`; while loading, the chart's area is `aria-busy` and the state is said once through a status region outside it. Every figure is your string, and the value axis always includes zero. A BarChart in a DataTable cell keeps its bars' boxes when one takes focus; wrap it in `data-table-control` to put it above the row's link for a pointer. Exports `BarChart`, `BarChartProps`, `BarChartSeries`, `BarSeries`, `BarValue`, `BarChartLabels`, `ChartStatus`, `ChartStatusLabels` and `ChartColor`. Its CSS is in `charts.css`, which `styles.css` already imports.

Charts 0.2.0 needs `@snapshot-ui/core` 0.9: its core peer moves to `^0.9.0`, since BarChart draws with rules and tokens only core 0.9 has. Upgrade `@snapshot-ui/core`, `@snapshot-ui/react` and `@snapshot-ui/charts` together.
