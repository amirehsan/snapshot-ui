Add UploadList and UploadTray: each file from waiting, uploading and reading to saved, needs review or failed, with its status in words and an icon, your `reason`, a progress bar with your `progressLabel` (one that slides while an upload has no `progress` and while a file is read), a link to its document through your RouterProvider once it has an `href`, and Retry, Remove or Cancel where you pass `onRetry`, `onRemove` or `onCancel`. A name keeps its extension in view. Focus never falls to the page as rows and buttons leave, and is never pulled back once the reader has moved it. The tray is a 28rem panel in the bottom end corner from 768px, with a title that counts the running batch ("Uploading 2 of 5") and then names what settled ("3 uploads complete, 1 failed, 1 needs review"), a fold button and a close button; under 768px it is a bar above the tab bar that opens a sheet, with Clear or Cancel uploads at its foot and a Clear on the bar once everything has settled, and the page keeps room for the bar under its content. It says three things politely: a batch starting, the files that failed or need review, and a summary once all have settled, never a percentage. Once nothing is in flight, the panel's Close and the sheet's footer button, which then reads "Clear", call `onClear`; while files are waiting or uploading, Close and the footer's "Cancel uploads" ask "Cancel 3 uploads?" before `onCancelAll`; a file being read is on your server and is not cancellable. Once hydrated, a control that focus lands on under the open or folded panel, or under the phone's bar, is scrolled 16px clear of it. `warnsOnUnload`, on by default, warns before the page unloads while files are waiting or uploading. The library never uploads: hold the items in your root layout and mount the tray in AppShell's `floating` slot. The tray writes its height as `--upload-tray-offset`, and the toasts now sit above it, above a sticky save bar or bulk-action bar and above the tab bar, in one column. Exports `UploadList`, `UploadTray` and the types `UploadItem`, `UploadStatus`, `UploadCounts`, `UploadListProps`, `UploadListLabels`, `UploadTrayProps` and `UploadTrayLabels`. `upload-tray.css` in `styles.css`.
