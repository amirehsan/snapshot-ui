Add Prose and ProseScroll for long-form text you render, such as a monthly report written in Markdown. The `prose` class, or the `Prose` wrapper (`as` a div, an article or a section), gives headings, paragraphs, lists, links, quotes, code, tables, figures and rules the library's type and spacing, with text at 70ch (`--prose-measure`). Every library component inside it, a chart, a table, a trail or a card included, draws as it does anywhere: prose's rules sit in a layer under the components' own, and a component is handed back to what it would draw outside prose. `not-prose` exempts a subtree of your own markup. GFM's footnotes draw small and slate with their label for screen readers alone, and task lists draw no markers. Links and images you turn into plain text have classes of their own: `prose-unlinked` keeps the text's look, and `prose-image` shows the alt text in a small box. `ProseScroll` is a named, focusable region that lets a wide table scroll on a phone. Both are static, so a server component renders a report with no client JavaScript. On a Persian page, code stays left to right, headings drop their tracking and ordered lists count in Persian digits. Load Sofia Sans's italic as well, since emphasis draws in it. `prose.css` is in `styles.css`.
