Rounded parts inside rounded parts now take concentric corners: a part in a holder's corner takes the holder's radius less the gap between them, its padding and its edge, so the two curves share a center in every shape. Menu, listbox and command palette rows are 15px in a 24px popup (3px in Soft), and a popup's filter field matches them; tabs are concentric with their track, the bulk bar's and a toast's buttons with their bar, the field button with its field (6px in Soft), and MultiSelect's tags with their frame. A card or a StatTile in a card is 7px in Rounded (19px compact, 6px in Soft) instead of 24px, a dialog's last action and a side panel's last footer action take the panel's concentric corner on the corner they share, and a line chart that ends a card does the same for its toggle. Card, Dialog, Drawer, Sheet, SidePanel, Popover, CommandPalette, Toast, the bulk bar and TabList now name their corner, edge and padding as `--holder-radius`, `--holder-border` and `--holder-pad`, so an app can give its own content in their corners the same corner: `max(0px, calc(var(--holder-radius) - var(--holder-border) - var(--holder-pad)))`. A field's corner is `--input-radius`, which its field button reads.
