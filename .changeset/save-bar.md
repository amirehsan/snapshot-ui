Add SaveBar: a sticky footer for a form or a review, the last child of the fields it saves. Your `message` says what will be saved and names the bar, `isDirty` adds the clay Unsaved changes chip and a "Discard changes?" question before `onCancel`, `actionLabel` and `onAction` are your primary action (a returned promise keeps it pending and Cancel waiting; `isPending` does the same for work you track), `secondaryActions` sit between them, and `status` with `statusTone` is a state said politely, such as a failed save, said again on each press. It is always shown and never disables its action. On a narrow bar the buttons take a line of their own under the sentence, side by side and sharing it; a button wraps to its own line only when its label is too long. Once hydrated, a field that focus lands on anywhere on the page under the bar, its description and error line with it, is scrolled 16px clear of it, and of a phone's upload bar above it, in a box that scrolls too, while focus on the bar's own buttons never moves the page. It warns before the page unloads while dirty; client links are not guarded. The bar writes its height as `--save-bar-offset` once hydrated (a token value before), which lifts the upload tray and the toasts above it at every width. A successful save is your own success toast. Exports `SaveBar` and the types `SaveBarProps` and `SaveBarLabels`. `save-bar.css` in `styles.css`.
