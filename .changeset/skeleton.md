Add Skeleton: a still shape where content will be, so a loading page keeps its layout. A `shape` of `line` (the default), the height of the text it stands for, `block` or `circle`, sized with `width` and `height` as CSS lengths. It pulses gently over 1.6s and stands still under reduced motion. It is hidden from assistive technology, so say "Loading" once in a status of your own, outside the part that is busy. It is static, so a server component renders it. Exports `Skeleton` and the type `SkeletonProps`. `skeleton.css` in `styles.css`.
