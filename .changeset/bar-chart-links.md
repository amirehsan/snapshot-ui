Link BarChart's bars. Give a `BarValue` an `href`, one per bar of every series, a stack's segments included, and that bar becomes a real anchor to it, named by its table cells as before and announced as a link rather than a bar. The hrefs are data, so a server component passes them with the figures: the server's HTML holds the links, each a stop of its own in drawn order until the chart hydrates, when they become its one roving stop, and a link a reader focused before then keeps focus and becomes the stop. A press anywhere in a bar's band follows its link through a target over the band, `a.bar-chart-hit`, hidden from assistive technology and never a stop: lying down the whole row from its name to its amount, standing up the band's column, the half nearer each bar in a band of two, and each segment's own run in a stack. Since the target is a real anchor, a click with Command, Control or Shift, a middle click and the context menu open a new tab or window as any link does. The readout names the bar under the pointer, so it says what a press opens; a tap follows the link and a touch held half a second shows the readout and follows nothing. Enter follows the focused bar's link and Space does nothing, so it never scrolls the page. The table's category cells link too with one series, and each figure's cell with two or more.

`navigate` takes your router's navigate, the function your `RouterProvider` has, since the charts import no React Aria: a plain click or Enter on a bar or a table link goes through it, and a modified click stays the browser's. `onBarPress(category, series)` is called with the band's and the series' index on every press, a link's before it is followed; a bar with no `href` in a chart with `onBarPress` is a button, which Enter and Space press. In a band of two bars without links, a pointer between or beside them now takes the nearer bar, as the targets do, where it took the band's first. `charts.css` gains the target's rules, and `data-table.css` leaves the target out of the cell controls it raises, as it leaves the bar.
