Add DataTable: rows of data from `items` and column definitions from `columns`, where every cell and every string is yours; the library formats no date, amount or count. Name it with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, which the types require. Sorting is controlled (`sortDescriptor`, `onSortChange`), ascending then descending with no unsorted state, and a column's `firstSortDirection` sets its first press. A row opens through `getRowHref` as a real link stretched over the row, through your `RouterProvider`, so a middle click opens a new tab; a press and Enter open and never select, except in the list face's Select mode, where a tap on an item selects it and Enter still opens it. `currentKey` marks the row whose detail is open, with a bar, a bolder name and `aria-current`. Selection is controlled from your page, never the URL (`selectedKeys`, and `onSelectionChange` always receives an array of the given rows' keys, never `'all'`), with a checkbox column, X, Space and Shift with a press for a range, `disabledKeys` with `disabledBehavior`, and a bulk-action bar (`bulkActions`, its count through `formatCount`) stuck to the bottom of the viewport; keys of rows that left `items` are counted out of the bar and warned about once. J and K move as the arrows, Page Up and Page Down by the rows in view, and letters never move focus. The header sticks under your page's header: set `--page-sticky-top` to its height, and to `0px` on an element of yours that scrolls around a table. `align: 'end'` sets figures, `total` adds a totals row (name a page's total "Page total" through `labels`), `overflow: 'wrap'` clamps a column at two lines, `isHeaderHidden` hides a header and keeps its name, and `hideBelow: 'md'` drops a column under 48rem of the table's own box. Under 40rem of its box the rows are a list, both faces in the server's HTML: each column goes where its `listSlot` says, `getGroup` groups the rows under a heading per day, which you pass only while sorted by date, and with selection on a Select button shows the checkboxes. `emptyState` takes an EmptyState; `isLoading` draws skeleton rows and says "Loading" once, and keeps the rows already shown while the next page loads; `error` takes the body or a line under the rows and wins over both. Render it from a client component: its props are functions. React Aria announces sorts and selection changes in its own English words, a Persian page included. Exports `DataTable` and the types `DataTableProps`, `DataTableBaseProps`, `DataTableColumn`, `DataTableLabels`, `DataTableSelectable` and `DataTableShared`. `data-table.css` in `styles.css`.
