Add the chart palette: `--color-chart-1` to `--color-chart-4` (teal, violet, olive and magenta), with light and dark values, for charts of several peers such as a stack of categories, and slate for "Other". Each is at least 3:1 against the canvas, lifted and float surfaces in both modes, clear of every color that carries a meaning, and checked for color-blind readers on neighbouring slots; a test keeps them so. Never use them for text or for a state. In Tailwind they are `bg-chart-1` to `bg-chart-4`.
